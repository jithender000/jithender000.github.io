Keep important documents in one place – and get reminded before they matter

Save Files & Set Reminders is a free Android app for keeping documents, photos, notes, voice notes and links in folders, with alarm-style reminders for renewals, bill dates and expiry dates. When a reminder rings, the alarm opens the exact item.

Features

Document organisation

Add PDFs, photos, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files. Rename, move, mark favourites and search by name.

Folders

Create folders for home papers, bills, insurance, college or work, and set a reminder on a whole folder.

Reminders that ring

One-time, daily, chosen weekdays, monthly or yearly. Full-screen alarm on the lock screen, your own sound, snooze interval and repeat count.

Open from the alarm

When a reminder rings, tap to open the exact document, note, voice note or link it belongs to.

Notes

Write notes, attach one or more photos and add a reminder to any note.

Voice notes

Record a voice note or import an audio file, name it and set a reminder to listen later.

Photo and image reminders

Keep a photo of a bill, form or warranty card as a document or inside a note, and get reminded before the date.

Link reminders

Save a web link – the page title is added for you – and set a reminder to come back to it.

Share and tidy up

Select several items, share documents, notes (as PDF), voice notes and links, or share a whole folder as a ZIP file.

How it works

  1. Add a document, photo, note, voice note or link and put it in a folder.
  2. Tap the bell to set a reminder: date, time and how often it repeats.
  3. When it rings, open the item from the alarm, snooze it or cancel it.

How your data is handled

  • Files stay on your phone. Documents, photos and recordings are copied into the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Uninstalling the app removes them from that phone.
  • Item details are kept in your private cloud record. Names, folders, note text, links and reminder settings are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under a private account the app creates for you, so they survive a reinstall. Security rules allow only that account to read them. Signing in with Google (optional) links this to your Google account.
  • Backup is manual. Tap Backup Now to copy your items to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (it uses your Google storage). Nothing goes to Drive unless you tap it. Restore needs the same Google account and never deletes what is already on the phone.
  • No ads, nothing sold. Technical performance data is collected by Firebase Performance Monitoring; your documents, notes and recordings are not part of it.
  • Delete any time. Profile → Delete Account removes your cloud records, the files on that phone, scheduled reminders and your Drive backup.

Common questions

Is the app free?

Yes – free on Google Play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is backup automatic?

No. Backup is manual: tap Backup Now after important changes. Without a backup, files on a lost or reset phone cannot be recovered.

Will reminders ring when the phone is locked?

Yes, as a full-screen alarm, once you allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders". Some phones' battery settings can delay alarms; allow the app to run in the background if asked.

Is it a file manager?

No. A file manager shows files already on your phone. This app keeps copies of the things that matter, in folders, with reminders.

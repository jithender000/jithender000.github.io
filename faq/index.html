Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what the app does – and what it does not do. Last checked against app version 1.0.9 (November 2026).

Organising documents

How can I organise important documents on my Android phone?

Put each kind of document in its own folder, give files clear names, and keep the copies in one app instead of scattered across the gallery, Downloads and chats. In Save Files & Set Reminders you add PDFs, photos, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text files, sort them into folders such as Home Papers, Insurance or College, rename or move them, mark favourites and search by name.

Which file types can I add?

PDFs, photos and other images, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and text files. Audio files can be imported as voice notes. Video files are not supported.

Can I search inside my PDFs?

No. Search finds documents by name. The app does not read the text inside PDFs (no OCR), so give important files clear names.

Can I scan documents with the camera?

Not inside the app. Take the photo with your camera or a scanner app first, then add the image or PDF to a folder.

Is this a file manager?

No. A file manager browses the files already on your phone. This app keeps copies of the things that matter in its own folders, adds reminders to them, and opens the right item from the alarm. Many people use both. Read the comparison.

Reminders

How can I remember document expiry dates, such as insurance or a driving licence?

Set a reminder on the document itself a few weeks before the date, and make it repeat yearly if it renews every year. Open the document, tap the bell, choose the date, time and repeat. When it rings, the alarm opens that document. Step-by-step guide.

Which repeat options are there?

One-time, daily, selected weekdays, monthly and yearly. You can also choose the alarm sound, vibration, snooze length and how many times it snoozes, and switch a reminder off without deleting the item.

Will the reminder ring if my phone is locked or I am using it?

On a locked phone it shows a full-screen alarm; while you are using the phone it appears as a banner. Allow notifications and the "Alarms & reminders" permission. Some phones' battery optimisation can delay alarms, so allow the app to run in the background if your phone asks.

Can I set a reminder for a photo of a bill or a form?

Yes. Add the photo as a document or attach it to a note, then set a reminder on it – for example before a bill's due date or a form's last date.

Can I get reminded to come back to a link?

Yes. Save the link (the page title is added for you), set a reminder, and open the link straight from the alarm.

Is there a voice-note reminder?

Yes. Record a voice note or import an audio file, name it, and set a reminder to listen to it later. The microphone is used only while you record.

Backup, privacy and data

Where are my files stored?

Files you add are copied into the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. They are not uploaded to the app's cloud database.

What is stored in the cloud?

Item details – names, folders, note text, links and reminder settings – are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under a private account the app creates for you, so they are kept after a reinstall. Security rules allow only that account to read or change them. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Is backup automatic?

No. Backup is manual: sign in with Google and tap Backup Now in Profile. The app copies your files and item details to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which uses your Google storage. Back up again after important changes.

How do I move my documents to a new phone?

On the old phone, sign in with Google and tap Backup Now. On the new phone, install the app, sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore Data. Restore asks first and never deletes what is already on the phone. Only items that were backed up come back. Full guide.

Do I need a Google account?

No, you can use the app without signing in. Google sign-in is needed for Backup Now and Restore Data and links your items to your Google account.

Does the app show ads or sell my data?

No ads, and your information is not sold or shared for advertising. Firebase Performance Monitoring collects technical performance data (such as app start time and device model); it does not include your documents, notes or recordings.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete Account permanently deletes your cloud records, the files of your items on that phone, scheduled reminders, your Google Drive backup and your account. You can also delete single items at any time.

About the app

Is Save Files & Set Reminders free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, with no ads and no in-app purchases (November 2026).

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Spanish, French and German. Change it in Profile → Language.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. The app is for Android 7.0 or later and is available on Google Play.

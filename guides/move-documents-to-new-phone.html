Changing phones: back up and restore your documents

Two steps: Backup Now on the old phone, Restore Data with the same Google account on the new one. Backup is manual – if you skip step one, there is nothing to restore.

Step 1 – on the old phone

  1. Open Profile and sign in with Google.
  2. Tap Backup Now. The app copies your files and item details to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. This uses your Google storage.
  3. Wait until Last backup shows the current time.

Step 2 – on the new phone

  1. Install Save Files & Set Reminders from Google Play.
  2. Open Profile and sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Tap Restore Data. The app shows the backup date and asks before it starts.
  4. Documents, folders, notes, voice notes, links and reminders come back. Nothing already on the new phone is deleted.

Things to know

  • Backup is not automatic. Back up again after important changes.
  • Only items that were in the backup come back. Files added after the last backup stay on the old phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its files from that phone – back up first.
  • Your backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete your account in the app or remove the app's data from Drive.
